How can L’HIVER evolve from a winter accessories retailer into a desirable winter lifestyle brand?

L’HIVER is a winter accessories brand offering hats, scarves and gloves, inspired by Scottish heritage and the craftsmanship of Robert Mackie, a manufacturer established in 1845. Its premium pieces, made from 75% wool and 25% angora, stand out for their palette of over 85 colours, which represents one of the brand’s strongest visual assets. However, its identity currently lacks consistency: caught between Scottish heritage, colourful products, premium positioning and a lifestyle universe, its brand territory remains unclear. Despite prices ranging from €39 to €95, its premium positioning is not yet fully established. The challenge of the workshop is therefore to redefine its positioning and brand territory through a new art direction, photography and video direction, as well as a coherent social media and editorial strategy and brand narrative.

With the brand’s current visual identity and tone of voice lacking a clear direction — caught between a youthful, casual streetwear aesthetic and the premium quality of its products — we decided to develop an art direction that brings these two legitimate aspects of the brand together.

The objective is to fully embrace both sides of the brand by highlighting the premium quality of its products, Robert Mackie’s craftsmanship and the transparency of their origins, while also conveying the cool, accessible and inclusive spirit of streetwear.

Three types of posts :
- Split-screen product features : showcasing the products and their details.
- Lifestyle atmosphere : showing how the brand can be worn and incorporated into everyday life.
- Heritage and brand updates : highlighting the brand’s heritage, behind-the-scenes content and latest news.
