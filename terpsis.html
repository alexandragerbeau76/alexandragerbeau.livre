Stymfalia, a Greek dairy company specialising in organic products, plans to launch a new range of ice creams made from high-quality ingredients such as organic milk, carob, citrus fruits, Greek nuts and oats. The collection, named either « Just Ice Cream » or « Terpsis », requires a modern and distinctive visual identity, including a logo and a coherent packaging range. The aim is to break away from the company’s traditional image while highlighting the quality of its ingredients, its production expertise and its Greek origins. The design should be innovative, recognisable and attractive to consumers, with the possibility of drawing inspiration from the history and culture of the Stymphalia region.

Between fresh and luxury :
- Fresh colors to make a reference at the temperature and organic products from the product
- Typographie strict for the contrats
- Illustration of the flavor to make the consumer want
- Thin line for show modern vibe and expertise of the brand
- Bird symbol to show the traditionnal side of the brand, from Stymfalia
- Tale some part of the bird for the graphic element on the packaging
- Use blur and glass morphisme to express an atmosphere of freshness and create innovative and timeless packaging and communication.
